Day 1 — Leave by sea

Keep your main luggage at your Tokyo base and travel light. Reach Takeshiba early and check the live operation notice before boarding.

Day 2 — Live at island speed

Stay at a minshuku, walk or cycle, eat where residents eat, and leave room for Habushiura Beach or an outdoor hot spring.

Day 3 — Return with a buffer

Confirm the return sailing before breakfast. Avoid a fixed international flight immediately after this trip; sea conditions can change.
