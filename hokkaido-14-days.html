The route

Tokyo baseOaraiTomakomaiHokkaido base

Days 1–3 — Tokyo as infrastructure

Recover, do laundry and prepare a small ferry bag. Explore nearby neighborhoods without racing through attractions.

Days 4–5 — Oarai and the crossing

Reach Oarai with a generous check-in buffer. Choose a private cabin, eat onboard, sleep and arrive without an airport transition.

Days 6–11 — Build a Hokkaido base

Stay long enough to learn one area. Alternate a demanding day with a local bath, market, restaurant or coastal walk.

Days 12–14 — Leave room to return

Keep final days as a buffer for transport changes and reflection.
